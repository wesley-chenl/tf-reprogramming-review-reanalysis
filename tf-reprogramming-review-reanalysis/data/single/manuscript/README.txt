Single TF reanalysis of the Hammelman mouse data. Files contain the frozen top tens, target-specific reference ranks, status and union counts, component results, scale and replicate checks, and reconstructed conditional random baselines and shortlist overlap. The supplied manuscript archive did not contain its two advertised full-score gzip files. Top-50 summaries and common-pool counts are quoted from the earlier audit. Details and accessions are in the supplement.
